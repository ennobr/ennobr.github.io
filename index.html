Hej!

I am a PhD student working on security for cyber-physical systems (CPS) since late 2023. My current research is focused on confidentiality attacks in networked control systems. For that, I am investigating multiple tools, such as Kalman filtering or delayed system inversion, to assess the privacy of internal states in time-discrete systems. Future research will investigate machine learning models in the controller. I am also interested in all sorts of control & optimization problems.

Previously, I completed the automation engineering M.Sc. program at RWTH Aachen in Germany in 2023 while writing my master's thesis on carbon-aware compute job allocation at the Automatic Control Laboratory at ETH Zurich.

My main supervisor is Henrik Sandberg, and my co-supervisor is Karl Henrik Johansson. I am part of the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program (WASP) program.
